When the Waters Rise · Monday, November 9 · 4 to 5 PM Pacific

Ask the speakers

What would you like to ask Dr. Mahar Lagmay, Dr. Lisandro Claudio or Dr. Diana Martinez? The moderator will pick from these for the panel. No name needed, and you don't have to RSVP to ask.

Your question

A sentence or two is plenty, up to 600 characters. English or Filipino are both fine.

What happens to your question

  • A teammate reads every question. The moderator gets the ones we pick, and there may not be time for all of them.
  • Questions are never shown on this site, and never with a name.
  • Want to come? RSVP here. It takes under a minute.

Organized by Gregor Posadas, Noam Anglo and Veronica Sison at UC Berkeley, with Rapha Felipe at Stanford, and co-hosted with PhilDev, the Philippine Development Foundation. Questions: see the FAQs, ask any of us on the day, or reply to your RSVP email.
