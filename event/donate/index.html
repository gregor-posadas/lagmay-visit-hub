When the Waters Rise

Bayanihan for typhoon relief

Alongside the event, we are raising funds for the Bayanihan Response for Disaster Relief, run by NAFCON, the National Alliance for Filipino Concerns. It supports families in the Philippines hit by Typhoon Tino and Super Typhoon Uwan. Any amount helps.

How to give

Scan the code with your phone camera, or use the button. It opens NAFCON's own donation page, where you can give through PayPal in any amount.

Donate to the Bayanihan campaign (opens NAFCON's site)

Online donations go straight to NAFCON through their page. For receipts or questions about a gift, contact NAFCON through the campaign page.

Why Bayanihan

BayanihanThe Filipino tradition of neighbors coming together to help one another, pictured as a whole village carrying a family's house on their shoulders to its new home. is the spirit of a community carrying each other through hard times. When the Waters Rise is about why the Philippines keeps flooding and what people here in the Bay Area can do. Helping the families still recovering from these typhoons is one thing we can do right now.

  • Typhoon Tino swept across the Visayas and central Philippines. NAFCON reports 224 lives lost and 109 people missing.
  • Super Typhoon Uwan forced the evacuation of over a million people, and its floods damaged homes, livelihoods and farmland.
