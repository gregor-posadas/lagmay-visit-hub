The map as a list

Each figure is one person who RSVPed, placed in their Bay Area county and, if they chose one, the Philippine province they are connected to. No names, and no towns. The map updates about once a minute.

The speakers

  • Dr. Mahar Lagmay

    Executive Director of the University of the Philippines Resilience Institute, and leader of Project NOAH, the Philippines' nationwide flood hazard mapping and warning program.

  • Dr. Lisandro Claudio

    UC Berkeley historian of the modern Philippines: its politics, culture and liberal thought.

  • Dr. Diana Martinez

    UC Berkeley architectural historian who studies how the United States built and shaped the Philippine Islands.

The same morning, Dr. Lagmay also gives a guest lecture in Development Engineering 203.

About the map and your answers

  • Your name and email are only for the RSVP list. They never appear here or at the event.
  • The map shows counts by Bay Area county and Philippine province. If you pick "Prefer not to say", you are counted but not placed.
  • If you share a flood story and say yes to sharing it, a teammate reads it first. A few may be read aloud at the event, never with a name.
  • Province boundaries: PSA (2023), via philippines-json-maps. Bay Area counties: click_that_hood. Floods named on the night come from the BahaWatch flood history.
