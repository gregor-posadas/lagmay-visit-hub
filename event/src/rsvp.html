What the form asks

  1. Your name and email. Required. Only for the RSVP list and event updates, never shown.
  2. How you'll join. In person at Banatao Auditorium, online on the livestream, or not sure yet.
  3. Where you live now. Just your Bay Area county, or "Outside the Bay Area", or "Prefer not to say".
  4. Your connection to the Philippines, if any. Optional, and no connection is needed to come. If you have one, you can pick the province it's to.
  5. A flood your family still talks about. Optional. You choose whether we may share it at the event, never with your name.
  6. A question for the speakers and anything you need to take part fully, like live captions or wheelchair seating. Both optional.

What happens to your answers

  • Your name and email stay with the organizing team. They never appear on this site or at the event.
  • The map of who is coming shows only counts, by Bay Area county and Philippine province. No names, and no towns.
  • Stories are read by a teammate first, and only shared if you said yes.
  • Questions? See Questions, or email us.
