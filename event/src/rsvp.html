What the form asks

  1. Your name and email. Required. Only for the RSVP list and event updates, never shown.
  2. How you'll join. Pick one: Dr. Lagmay's guest lecture on the UC Berkeley campus on November 9 from 11 AM to 12 PM, the panel in Banatao Auditorium on the UC Berkeley campus from 4 to 5 PM, his guest lecture on the Stanford campus on November 10, both the morning lecture and the panel, the livestream of the panel, or not sure yet. The morning lecture has room for 20 guests on top of the class; the panel has 149 seats, and the reception after it is for the same 149. Spots go in the order people RSVP, and each limited session has a waitlist once it is full.
  3. Which Bay Area county you live in. Or "Outside the Bay Area", or "Prefer not to say".
  4. Your connection to the Philippines, if any. Optional, and no connection is needed to come. If you have one, you can pick the province it's to.
  5. A flood your family still talks about. Optional. You choose whether we may share it at the event, never with your name.
  6. A question for the speakers and anything you need to take part fully, like live captions or wheelchair seating. Both optional.
  7. Food allergies or dietary restrictions, for the refreshments after the panel. Optional, and only the organizers see it.
  8. Whether you'd like to connect with others working on projects in or for the Philippines. Optional. If yes, the form links you to Connect, where you can add a card.

What happens to your answers

  • Your name and email stay with the organizing team. They never appear on this site or at the event.
  • The map of who is coming shows only counts, by Bay Area county and Philippine province. No names, and no towns.
  • Stories are read by a teammate first, and only shared if you said yes.
  • Questions? See Questions, or email us.
