What the form asks

  1. Your name and email. Required. Only for the RSVP list and event updates, never shown.
  2. Which you'll join. Select all that apply: the DevEng 203 guest lecture (11 AM to 12 PM, B100 Blum Hall), the discussion panel in Banatao Auditorium or online (4 to 5 PM), and the reception and networking after (from 5 PM, B100 Blum Hall). All on Monday, November 9. Lecture and panel spots go in the order people RSVP, with a waitlist once one is full.
  3. Where you live. Your Bay Area county, or elsewhere in California, the US, the Philippines or another country. "Prefer not to say" is a choice too.
  4. Need an accommodation, like live captions or wheelchair seating, or have a food allergy? Email gregorposadas@berkeley.edu.
  5. A question for the speakers. Optional. The moderator picks from these, never with names.
  6. Your connection to the Philippines, if any, including a friend or someone close to you from there. No connection is needed to come. If your family is from there or you've lived there, you can pick the province it's to.
  7. That's it. You can submit there, or add a few optional extras: a flood your family still talks about (you choose whether we may share it, never with your name), and whether you'd like to connect with others working on projects in or for the Philippines.

What happens to your answers

  • Your name and email stay with the organizing team. They never appear on this site or at the event.
  • The map of who is coming shows only counts, by Bay Area county and Philippine province. No names, and no towns.
  • Stories are read by a teammate first, and only shared if you said yes.
  • Questions? See Questions, or email us.
