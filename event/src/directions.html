When the Waters Rise · Monday, November 9 · 3 to 5 PM

Directions

The talk is in Banatao Auditorium, on the main level of Sutardja Dai Hall, on the north side of the UC Berkeley campus.

  • By BARTGet off at Downtown Berkeley. From there it's a 15 to 20 minute walk, gently uphill, to Hearst and LeRoy on the north side of campus.
  • By car or rideshareFor drop-off, use 2594 Hearst Avenue at LeRoy Avenue. The nearest pay garage is Lower Hearst (enter from Scenic Avenue), about a 10 minute walk east along Hearst. Metered street parking nearby is limited. Campus visitor parking.
  • From Stanford and the PeninsulaTake Caltrain to Millbrae, then BART to Downtown Berkeley, and allow about two hours. Or join the livestream; the link goes to everyone who RSVPs.
  • Once you're thereEnter from Hearst Avenue. The auditorium is just past the entrance on the main level, with elevators inside. If you need help getting in or around, email us or see accessibility.
