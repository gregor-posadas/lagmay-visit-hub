Connect around Philippine projects

Working on something in or for the Philippines, or hoping to? Add a short card so people at the event, watching online or in the Philippines can find you. Before November 9 we can also email you introductions to people with interests like yours.

How it works

  1. Add a card. A short form asks what you work on, what you are looking for, and whether to show your card here or only use it for introductions. It takes about three minutes.
  2. An organizer reads it. Every card is read before it appears. Your email is never shown on this page.
  3. Find each other. Approved cards appear below. If you asked for introductions, you will get an email before November 9 with people who share your interests and also asked to be introduced.

Anyone is welcome, and you don't need to RSVP to the talk to add a card. To change or remove your card, email gregorposadas@berkeley.edu.
