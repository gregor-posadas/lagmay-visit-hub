When the Waters Rise

Table at the reception

Part of a Filipino student org or community group? Have a table at the reception right after the panel, so people can meet you and learn what you're working on.

Sign up to table (opens a Google Form)

When and where

  • Monday, November 9, 5 to 6 PM, right after the panel in Banatao Auditorium.
  • B100 Blum Hall at UC Berkeley, next door to Sutardja Dai Hall. See Directions.
  • The reception is free and open to everyone who comes to the event, with food, the speakers and lots of time to talk.

How to sign up

  1. Fill out the form. It asks for a contact person, your organization, why you'd like to table and how many chairs you need. It takes a couple of minutes.
  2. We'll save you a spot and follow up by email with the details before the day.
  3. Bring what tells your story, like flyers, sign-up sheets or a poster about your work.

Your members are welcome at the panel too. Ask them to RSVP, since in-person seats are limited. Questions about tabling? Contact us.
