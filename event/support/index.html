When the Waters Rise

Support the event

The talk is free and open to everyone. Bringing Dr. Lagmay here from Manila is not. Here is what it costs, what is already covered, and how you can help close the gap.

Sponsor sheet (PDF, one page)Email us

Where the money stands

  • Still neededDr. Lagmay's round-trip flight from Manila to San Francisco, about $1,500. This is the one large cost no one has covered yet, and the reason we are asking.
  • Still neededLight refreshments after the talk, about $500, so people can stay and keep talking.
  • Applied forThe auditorium, speaker honoraria, local transport and printing, through the ASUC Student Opportunity Fund. We expect a decision soon.
  • CoveredRecording and livestreaming, thanks to the Townsend Center for the Humanities.

Student funds at Berkeley cannot pay for international travel or food, which is why those two are still open.

Ways to help

Sponsor the flight or the refreshments

For departments, research centers, student organizations, Filipino community groups and businesses. Any amount helps, and a one-way fare alone would make the visit possible.

Sponsors are thanked on this site and on the event slides, from the stage, in the recording, and in the short brief we write afterwards.

Bring food

If you run a Filipino restaurant or bakery in the Bay Area and would like to provide merienda after the talk, we would love to feature you.

Co-host or spread the word

Student and community organizations can co-host and help us reach people who care about this. Everyone can share the event, and the Share button at the top of this page makes an Instagram story in one tap.

Help on the day

We need a few people to welcome guests, run the microphone for questions and help with the livestream on November 9.

Get in touch

Email Gregor Posadas at gregorposadas@berkeley.edu and tell us how you would like to help.
