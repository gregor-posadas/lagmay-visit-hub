Ways to help

Sponsor the event

For departments, research centers, student organizations, Filipino community groups and businesses. Cover one cost on the list, like the room or the speakers' honoraria, or give any amount toward the whole.

Sponsors are thanked on this site and on the event slides, from the stage, in the recording, and in the short brief we write afterwards.

Bring food

If you run a Filipino restaurant or bakery in the Bay Area and would like to provide meriendaA Filipino afternoon snack, often shared. after the talk, we would love to feature you.

Co-host or spread the word

Student and community organizations can co-host and help us reach people who care about this. Everyone can share the event, and the Share button at the top of this page makes an Instagram story in one tap.

Help on the day

We need a few people to welcome guests, run the microphone for questions and help with the livestream on November 9.

Get in touch

Email Gregor Posadas at gregorposadas@berkeley.edu and tell us how you would like to help.
