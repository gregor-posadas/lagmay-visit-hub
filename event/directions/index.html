The room

Banatao Auditorium is on the main level of Sutardja Dai Hall. It holds 149 people, with a low stage, a podium, microphones and a large screen.

After the talk, we will walk over together to room B100 in Blum Hall, right next door, to meet the speakers. Blum Hall is joined to Sutardja Dai Hall by plazas and bridges. Food and drinks are not allowed in the auditorium, so anything to eat will be there.
