The cards

Sample cards. These people are made up, to show how the cards will look.

    How it works

    1. Add a card. A short form asks what you work on, what you are looking for, and whether to show your card here or only use it for introductions. It takes about three minutes.
    2. An organizer reads it. Every card is read before it appears. Your email is never shown on this page.
    3. Find each other. Approved cards appear at the top of this page. If you asked for introductions, you will get an email before November 9 with people who share your interests and also asked to be introduced.

    Anyone is welcome, and you don't need to RSVP to the talk to add a card. To change or remove your card, email gregorposadas@berkeley.edu.

    Organized by Gregor Posadas, Noam Anglo and Veronica Sison at UC Berkeley, with Rapha Felipe at Stanford, and co-hosted with PhilDev, the Philippine Development Foundation. Questions: see Questions, ask any of us on the day, or reply to your RSVP email.
